AuthSync Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

AuthSync is a Safari Web Extension published by Dvlprr, LLC (Andrae Crumbly). The Mac app exists only to install and register the extension.

Data AuthSync does not collect

AuthSync does not have an account system and does not operate a backend. It does not transmit page content, cookies, passwords, or browsing history to the developer or to any third party. It makes no network requests of its own.

Data stored on your Mac

Settings you enter (watched hostnames, login hosts, refresh mode, and related toggles), a last-sync summary, and an optional activity log are stored in the browser’s local extension storage. The activity log records host names only, never paths or query strings. You can clear the log from the popup. A short-lived queue of tab IDs may be kept in session storage while “When I open each tab” is enabled.

Permissions

AuthSync reads tab URLs so it can match hostnames you saved and reload those tabs. It observes top-frame navigations and main-frame redirects to detect a completed login. It requests access to all websites because the hostnames are user-defined and cannot be listed in advance. Safari still lets you limit that access per site.
